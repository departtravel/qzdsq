# WHY TUNISIA FOR YOUR COMMUNITY

Tunisia is compact enough to combine wildly different landscapes in a single short trip, yet still genuinely off the mass-tourism radar inland. Within a few hours of Djerba a traveller can move from palm-shaded mountain oases and dramatic canyons (Chebika, Tamerza, Midès) to the vast salt flats of Chott el Jerid and the dunes of the Sahara.

Threaded through that nature is living Amazigh (Berber) culture: troglodyte homes carved into the hills of Matmata and Tamezret, fortified granaries (ksour), oasis farming communities, and craft traditions like Guellala pottery. Travellers don't observe these communities from a coach window — they stay in their guesthouses, share their meals, and buy directly from their makers.

For a platform built on nature and community-based tourism, Tunisia offers a rare combination: authentic rural encounters, world-class landscapes, and short flight times from Germany — all in a destination most of your community has never considered.

# Sample Itineraries

## Rural Berber & Sahara CBT — ~5 Days

**Overview.** A compact community-based module built for independent travellers who want the essence of the Berber south and the Sahara without committing to a long fixed tour. It slots neatly onto either end of a Djerba stay and leans entirely on locally-owned guesthouses and hosts.

**Key Information.**
- Duration: 5 days / 4 nights
- Start / end: Djerba (flexible; transfers à la carte)
- Group size: guaranteed from 2 pax
- Style: rural CBT, homestays & eco-lodge, light walking, optional camel
- Pace: relaxed, independent-friendly

**Highlights.**
- Troglodyte villages of Matmata and Tamezret with a Berber family home visit
- Overnight in a restored troglodyte guesthouse
- Mountain oases and canyons of Chebika, Tamerza and Midès by 4x4
- Chott el Jerid salt lake crossing and a night at an eco-minded desert camp
- Direct contact with rural hosts and artisans throughout

**Day-by-Day Itinerary.**

**Day 1: Djerba to the Berber Hills**
Leave the island and head inland into the Dahar plateau, where the landscape turns from coast to rugged stone hills. The afternoon is spent exploring fortified granaries (ksour) and settling into a restored troglodyte guesthouse, sharing an evening meal cooked by your hosts.
![Rural Berber village](images/berber-village-1.jpg)
*Accommodation:* Restored troglodyte guesthouse (Tijma) · *Meals:* Dinner

**Day 2: Matmata & Tamezret Troglodyte Villages**
A full day among the underground Berber homes of Matmata and the hilltop village of Tamezret, including a welcome into a Berber family home for tea and conversation. You'll understand how these communities live with the heat and the land, and meet the people directly rather than passing through.
![Berber village life](images/berber-village-2.jpg)
*Accommodation:* Local *dar* (homestay-style) · *Meals:* Breakfast, lunch

**Day 3: Mountain Oases & Canyons by 4x4**
Travel west toward Tozeur and spend the day by 4x4 in the mountain oases of Chebika, Tamerza and Midès — palm-filled gorges, waterfalls and dramatic canyon walls on the old Algerian frontier. There is time to walk and to swim in a natural oasis pool.
![Mountain oasis](images/mountain-oasis.jpg)
*Accommodation:* Oasis guesthouse · *Meals:* Breakfast, dinner

**Day 4: Chott el Jerid & the Sahara**
Cross the shimmering salt flats of Chott el Jerid to reach the edge of the dunes. An optional camel trek (always your choice) leads to an eco-minded desert camp, where the evening brings local music around the fire under a sky full of stars.
![Desert camp under the stars](images/desert-camp-music.jpg)
*Accommodation:* Eco-minded desert camp (Dunes Insolites) · *Meals:* Breakfast, dinner

**Day 5: Desert Sunrise & Return**
Wake to sunrise over the dunes before a relaxed breakfast at camp. The day is given to the journey back toward Djerba, with stops to stretch your legs and a final glimpse of the gorge country behind you.
![Desert camp at dawn](images/desert-camp-setup.jpg)
*Accommodation:* — · *Meals:* Breakfast

**Highlights.**
- The fortified granary of Ksar Hadada and the ksour of the Dahar

Arrive on the island of Djerba and settle in. The afternoon introduces the island's gentle character — the El Ghriba synagogue, the potters of Guellala and the colourful street art of Djerbahood — before an evening meal at a locally-owned table.

**Day 2: Into the Ksour Country**
Cross to the mainland and climb into the Dahar plateau, home to the south's dramatic fortified granaries. Explore the layered courtyards of Ksar Hadada and meet the communities who still tend these structures, then settle into a restored troglodyte guesthouse.
![Ksar Hadada granary](images/ksar-hadada.jpg)
*Accommodation:* Restored troglodyte guesthouse (Tijma) · *Meals:* Breakfast, dinner

**Day 3: Matmata & Tamezret**
A full day among the underground Berber homes of Matmata and the hilltop village of Tamezret, including a visit to a Berber family home. You'll learn how the land shapes daily life here and spend time with hosts and craftspeople.

**Day 4: To the Oases of the South-west**
Travel west across changing country toward Tozeur, the great oasis town on the edge of the Sahara. The afternoon is spent walking the labyrinthine old town and the vast palm grove, with time to relax by an oasis pool.
![Oasis pool](images/oasis-pool.jpg)
*Accommodation:* Oasis guesthouse · *Meals:* Breakfast, dinner

**Day 5: Mountain Oases & Canyons 4x4**
By 4x4 into the mountains for Chebika, Tamerza and Midès — palm gorges, waterfalls and sheer canyon walls. There's time to walk the trails and take in the silence of these frontier oases.

**Day 6: Chott el Jerid & the Sahara**
Cross the immense salt flats of Chott el Jerid to reach the dunes. An optional camel trek (always your choice) carries you to an eco-minded desert camp for an evening of local music and a night under the stars.
![Desert camp setup](images/desert-camp-setup.jpg)

Sunrise over the dunes, then a gradual return through the dramatic oasis-and-canyon landscapes of the south-west, with stops to take in the views and the palm-fringed valleys before reaching your overnight base.
![Tamerza palace and oasis](images/tamerzapalace.jpg)

**Day 8: Kairouan on the Way North**
Journey toward Kairouan, Tunisia's holy city, to walk the Great Mosque and the medina and meet carpet and craft workshops. From here you continue to your departure point, or extend with our Roman or coastal modules.

# COMMUNITY-BASED & RESPONSIBLE TRAVEL

Community-based tourism is not a label we added on — it is how this product is built.

- **Locally owned.** Roughly 95% of the accommodation we use and about 90% of the meals served are from locally-owned businesses, keeping the value of each booking in the communities your travellers visit.
- **Fair, direct pay.** Artisans and rural hosts are paid fairly and directly, not through layers of intermediaries.
- **Animal welfare.** Our standards are ABTA-aligned; camel riding is always optional and never required to enjoy the experience.
- **Environment.** We use reusable water, favour eco-minded accommodation such as the Dunes Insolites camp, and can supply CO₂e-per-night figures — we are eCollective-ready for carbon reporting on your platform.
- **Genuine encounters.** Homestays, troglodyte guesthouses and family-home visits mean travellers meet communities as guests, not spectators.
