# WHY TUNISIA — THE OFFBEAT NORTH AFRICA

Tunisia is the North African destination your clients have not yet been sold. It packs the Sahara, the Berber highlands, Mediterranean islands and some of the finest Roman ruins outside Italy into distances you can cross by road in a single relaxed trip — no domestic flights, no dawn transfers.

While the coast draws the package crowds, the interior stays quiet. At Dougga and Sbeitla your travellers can walk a Roman forum almost alone. In the south, the ksour, oases and dunes deliver the cinematic, lesser-known Tunisia that rewards the curious independent traveller — the offbeat heart of a country that is genuinely safe, warm and welcoming, and easy to reach from the UK.

For an operator actively developing North Africa, Tunisia is the standout: differentiated, deliverable, and ready for tailor-made FIT product now.

## Berber South & Sahara — Beyond the Map

**Overview.** Our most offbeat journey: a south-focused loop from Djerba through the ksour, troglodyte villages and mountain oases into the dunes of the Grand Erg Oriental, with a camel trek and a wild bivouac under the stars. Soft adventure with deep local immersion at every step.

**Key Information**

| | |
|---|---|
| Duration | 8–9 days |
| Style | Offbeat / soft adventure / immersive |
| Start / End | Djerba |
| Guaranteed from | 2 passengers |
| Basis | Land-only, twin-share, private/tailor-made |
| Walking | Gentle to moderate; camel riding optional |

**Highlights**

- Overnight in a restored troglodyte house at Tijma
- The ksour of the south — Ksar Hadada, Chenini, Douiret
- Camel trek and wild bivouac in the Grand Erg Oriental
- Mountain oases and canyons — Chebika, Tamerza, Midès
- Desert music and storytelling under the stars
- Djerba crafts — Guellala pottery and Djerbahood street art

**Day-by-Day Itinerary**

**Day 1: Arrival on Djerba**
Arrive on the island of Djerba and meet your English-speaking certified leader. Settle into a locally owned island *dar*, walk the whitewashed lanes and ease into Tunisian time with a welcome dinner of island specialities.
![Djerba — whitewashed island lanes](images/djerba.jpeg)
*Accommodation:* Island *dar* (locally owned) · *Meals:* Dinner

**Day 2: Djerba Crafts & Djerbahood**
A full immersive day on Djerba: throw a pot with the potters of Guellala, visit the ancient El Ghriba synagogue, and wander the open-air murals of Djerbahood, meeting the artists who keep the village alive.
![Djerbahood — open-air street art](images/djerbahood.jpg)
*Accommodation:* Island *dar* (locally owned) · *Meals:* Breakfast, Lunch

**Day 3: Into the Ksour — Ksar Hadada & Chenini**
Cross to the mainland and climb into the hill country of the south. Explore the fortified granaries of Ksar Hadada and the dramatic hilltop village of Chenini, where Berber families still live among the ridgelines.
![Ksar Hadada — fortified granary of the south](images/ksar-hadada.jpg)
*Accommodation:* Restored troglodyte house, Tijma · *Meals:* Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner

**Day 4: Troglodyte Country — Matmata & Tamezret**
Spend the day in the Berber highlands among the underground homes of Matmata and the stone village of Tamezret, sharing mint tea and conversation in a Berber family home before returning to your troglodyte guesthouse.
![Berber highland village](images/berber-village-1.jpg)
*Accommodation:* Restored troglodyte house, Tijma · *Meals:* Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner

**Day 5: Camel Trek & Wild Bivouac**
Drive to the edge of the Grand Erg Oriental and trade wheels for camels (riding always optional — walk if you prefer). Trek into the dunes to a wild bivouac, where dinner is followed by desert music and storytelling under a vast sky.
![Camel trek to the wild bivouac](images/desert-camp-setup.jpg)
*Accommodation:* Wild desert bivouac · *Meals:* Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner

**Day 6: Dunes & the Mountain Oases**
Wake to sunrise over the sand, then journey to the mountain oases. Walk the palm-shaded canyons and waterfalls of Chebika and Tamerza, where springs cut through ochre cliffs.
![Mountain oasis and canyon](images/mountain-oasis.jpg)
*Accommodation:* Oasis hotel · *Meals:* Breakfast, Dinner

**Day 7: Midès Canyon & Oasis Life**
A gentle walk along the rim of the spectacular Midès canyon, then time among the palmeries to slow down, swim and meet oasis growers tending the date palms.
![Oasis pool among the palms](images/oasis-pool.jpg)
*Accommodation:* Oasis hotel · *Meals:* Breakfast, Dinner

**Day 8: Return to Djerba**
A scenic drive back toward the coast, with stops for the landscapes and villages along the way, returning to Djerba for a farewell dinner in a locally owned *dar*.
![Return to Djerba](images/djerba.jpeg)
*Accommodation:* Island *dar* (locally owned) · *Meals:* Breakfast, Dinner

**Day 9: Departure**
A relaxed final morning on the island before your transfer to Djerba airport. (Day 9 is optional — the journey can be tailored to 8 days.)
![Farewell to the south](images/dunesinsolites.webp)
*Accommodation:* — · *Meals:* Breakfast

# RESPONSIBLE TRAVEL

Responsible travel is structural for us, not a badge.

- **Local ownership.** Around 95% of our accommodation and roughly 90% of meals are locally owned — heritage *dars*, oasis hotels, a desert camp and troglodyte guesthouses run by the families who host you.
- **Fair, direct pay.** Artisans, hosts and guides are paid directly and fairly, so the value of each journey stays in the community your clients visit.
- **Animal welfare.** ABTA-aligned standards apply throughout; camel riding is always optional, never compulsory.
- **Lower-impact operations.** Reusable water on every trip, our own efficiently routed fleet, and CO₂e-per-night reporting that is eCollective-ready for your carbon accounting.
