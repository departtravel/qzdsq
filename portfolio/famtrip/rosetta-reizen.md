# WHY TUNISIA FOR YOUR TRAVELLERS

Since this is your first visit, a quick orientation. Tunisia packs an extraordinary range into short driving distances: world-class Roman sites (Dougga, El Jem, Carthage, Sbeitla, Bulla Regia), the holy city of Kairouan, living Berber culture in the Dahar, real Sahara within a day of the coast, and the island of Djerba with its weavers, potters and one of the oldest synagogues in the world. It is safe, welcoming, easy to combine culture with adventure and food, and — crucially for a developing programme — still genuinely uncrowded off the main coast.

## Berber South & Sahara — Beyond the Map

**Overview.** Eight to nine days through the part of Tunisia your clients will not find in a brochure: the fortified granaries (*ksour*) of the Dahar, the cliff-clinging Berber villages of Chenini and Douiret, the troglodyte dwellings of Matmata, and a camel-borne bivouac under the stars in the Grand Erg Oriental. Community contact and authentic stays are the spine of the trip.

**Key Information.** Land-only, twin-share · guaranteed from 2 participants · private, small-group or tailor-made · no compulsory single supplement · ONTT-certified English-speaking tour leader · own insured fleet · 24/7 in-country support.

**Highlights.** Chenini & Douiret hilltop villages · Ksar Hadada & Dahar ksour · troglodyte overnight at Au Trait d'Union, Tijma · Berber family home in Matmata · camel trek & wild bivouac in the Grand Erg Oriental · Berber music & storytelling at the camp · Djerba weavers and Guellala pottery.

### Day-by-Day Itinerary

**Day 1: Arrival in Djerba**
Welcome at Djerba airport and transfer to a locally-owned island *dar*. Time to settle in, with an orientation briefing from your tour leader over mint tea and a first taste of Djerban hospitality.
![Djerba Houmt Souk](images/djerba.jpeg)
*Accommodation:* Dar Dhiafa, Djerba · *Meals:* Dinner.

**Day 2: Djerba — weavers, potters and the medina**
A day with Djerba's living crafts: the weavers' workshops, the pottery kilns of Guellala, the lanes of Houmt Souk, and the colourful open-air murals of Djerbahood. Every stop pays artisans and hosts directly.
![Djerba street art at Djerbahood](images/djerbahood.jpg)
*Accommodation:* Dar Dhiafa, Djerba · *Meals:* Breakfast, lunch.

**Day 3: Into the Dahar — Ksar Hadada & the ksour**
Cross to the mainland and climb into the Dahar mountains to explore the fortified Berber granaries, with Ksar Hadada among the finest. We walk the ksour with a local guide who grew up among them.
![Berber ksar granary at Ksar Hadada](images/ksar-hadada.jpg)
*Accommodation:* Restored guesthouse, Dahar · *Meals:* Breakfast, lunch, dinner.

**Day 4: Chenini & Douiret**
A full day among the cliff-clinging Berber villages of Chenini and Douiret — terraced houses, underground mosques and sweeping valley views. Lunch is shared with a local family.
![Troglodyte Berber village in the valley](images/berber-village-2.jpg)
*Accommodation:* Restored guesthouse, Dahar · *Meals:* Breakfast, lunch, dinner.

**Day 5: Matmata — a Berber family home**
We descend to Matmata to stay overnight in a troglodyte dwelling carved into the earth, hosted by a Berber family. Evening conversation, home cooking and a window into daily mountain life.
![Berber village dwellings](images/berber-village-1.jpg)
*Accommodation:* Berber family troglodyte home, Matmata · *Meals:* Breakfast, lunch, dinner.

**Day 6: Into the Sahara — camel trek & wild bivouac**
Transfer to the dune edge near Douz, then mount up for a camel trek into the Grand Erg Oriental to a wild bivouac. Camel encounters are welfare-compliant and always optional. Dinner around the fire under a vast desert sky.
![Desert camp tents under the stars](images/desert-camp-setup.jpg)
*Accommodation:* Wild bivouac, Grand Erg Oriental · *Meals:* Breakfast, lunch, dinner.

**Day 7: Desert dawn & Berber music**
Sunrise over the dunes, a slow desert morning, and an evening of Berber music and storytelling around the campfire — the kind of unscripted, human moment your clients remember for years.
![Bedouin music around the campfire](images/desert-camp-music.jpg)
*Accommodation:* Sahara desert camp (Dunes Insolites) · *Meals:* Breakfast, lunch, dinner.

**Day 8: Troglodyte night at Tijma**
Leave the sand for the restored troglodyte house Au Trait d'Union at Tijma, a characterful CBT stay. A relaxed final day with time to absorb the journey before departure.
![Mountain oasis palm groves](images/mountain-oasis.jpg)
*Accommodation:* Au Trait d'Union, Tijma · *Meals:* Breakfast, dinner.

**Day 9: Departure**
Transfer to Djerba airport (or onward connection) for your flight home, with optional extensions to the eclipse-season desert product on request.
![Djerba Houmt Souk](images/djerba.jpeg)
*Accommodation:* — · *Meals:* Breakfast.

## Flavours of Tunisia — Culinary & Cultural

**Overview.** Seven to eight days following Tunisia through its kitchens and markets: cooking hands-on with families, meeting harissa and olive-oil producers, exploring the Andalusian heritage of Testour, and discovering the distinctive fusion cuisine of Djerba. A culture-first food journey for clients who travel to taste a place.

**Key Information.** Land-only, twin-share · guaranteed from 2 participants · private, small-group or tailor-made · no compulsory single supplement · ONTT-certified English-speaking tour leader · own insured fleet · 24/7 in-country support.

**Highlights.** Cooking with families · harissa & olive-oil producers · Andalusian Testour · Djerban fusion cuisine · Sidi Bou Saïd · Kairouan's makroudh · vineyard wine tasting near Hammamet · markets and family kitchens throughout.

### Day-by-Day Itinerary

**Day 1: Arrival in Tunis & Sidi Bou Saïd**
Welcome at Tunis airport and transfer to a boutique *dar*. An easy first evening in the blue-and-white village of Sidi Bou Saïd, with a sunset *bambalouni* and mint tea overlooking the bay.
![Sidi Bou Saïd blue and white](images/sidi-bou-said.jpg)
*Accommodation:* Boutique dar, Tunis · *Meals:* Dinner.

**Day 2: Tunis medina — markets & a family kitchen**
A morning through the medina's spice and produce souks, then a hands-on cooking session in a family kitchen: brik, slata mechouia and a tagine you sit down to share with your hosts.
![Boutique dar in Tunis](images/Dareljeld.jpg)
*Accommodation:* Boutique dar, Tunis · *Meals:* Breakfast, lunch.

**Day 3: Andalusian Testour & a vineyard near Hammamet**
Drive to Testour to taste the Andalusian heritage baked into its breads and pastries, then on to a vineyard near Hammamet for a wine tasting and a walk around the kasbah.
![Hammamet kasbah](images/hammamet.jpg)
*Accommodation:* Heritage dar, Hammamet · *Meals:* Breakfast, lunch, dinner.

**Day 4: Olive oil & the road south to Kairouan**
A visit to an olive-oil producer to follow the fruit from grove to press, then into Kairouan — Tunisia's holy city and home of the syrup-soaked makroudh, tasted fresh from the source.
![Kairouan](images/kasbaah-kairouan.jpg)
*Accommodation:* Heritage dar, Kairouan · *Meals:* Breakfast, lunch.

**Day 5: Harissa country & the southern oases**
Meet a harissa producer to understand Tunisia's defining condiment from chilli to jar, then continue toward the oasis country with a stop among the palm groves and a relaxed oasis-side lunch.
![Oasis pool among the palms](images/oasis-pool.jpg)
*Accommodation:* Oasis hotel (Tamerza Palace) · *Meals:* Breakfast, lunch, dinner.

**Day 6: To Djerba — the island table**
Cross to Djerba, the most distinctive food culture in the country, where Berber, Jewish and Arab influences meet. An afternoon market walk sets up the evening's island cooking session.
![Djerba Houmt Souk](images/djerba.jpeg)
*Accommodation:* Island dar (Dar Dhiafa) · *Meals:* Breakfast, dinner.

**Day 7: Djerban fusion cuisine**
A full day of Djerban cooking — the island's fish, octopus and date dishes — with a family who farm and fish what they cook. Time to wander Houmt Souk and Guellala before a farewell dinner.
![Djerba street art at Djerbahood](images/djerbahood.jpg)
*Accommodation:* Island dar (Dar Dhiafa) · *Meals:* Breakfast, lunch, dinner.

**Day 8: Departure**
A final island breakfast and transfer to Djerba airport, with optional extensions into the Berber south or the 2027 eclipse-season desert product on request.
![Sousse medina](images/sousse.webp)
*Accommodation:* — · *Meals:* Breakfast.
