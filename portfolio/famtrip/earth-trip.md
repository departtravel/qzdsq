# WHY TUNISIA — BEYOND THE BEACH

Tunisia packs an extraordinary range into a compact, easily-toured country. In a single tailor-made journey a client can stand in the Roman amphitheatre of El Jem, trace mosaics at the Bardo, sleep in a restored Berber troglodyte house, ride a camel into the Sahara to a camp beneath the stars, wander the holy medina of Kairouan, and finish among the whitewashed-and-blue lanes of Sidi Bou Saïd.

It is one of the most accessible and safe-feeling corners of North Africa, with short internal transfers, warm hospitality, and a cultural layering — Berber, Carthaginian, Roman, Arab, Ottoman, French — that few destinations of its size can rival. For an operator whose clients have "done" the obvious, Tunisia beyond the beach is a genuinely fresh, story-rich proposition.

## Grand Tunisia Journey — Private Luxury Tailor-Made (12 Days)

### Overview
Our flagship: a fully private, tailor-made circuit that draws the complete arc of Tunisia — Roman grandeur, holy cities, the Sahara, troglodyte Berber country, island heritage and the coast. Built for FIT couples and families who want depth, comfort and the freedom to set their own pace, with a Superior accommodation tier available throughout.

### Key Information
- **Duration:** 12 days / 11 nights
- **Style:** Private, tailor-made, chauffeured with a private ONTT-certified English-speaking guide
- **Group size:** Guaranteed from 2 passengers
- **Accommodation:** Boutique & heritage *dars*, oasis palace, upscale desert camp (Superior tier optional)
- **Best season:** October–April (and pleasant spring/autumn for the south)

### Highlights
- Carthage, the Bardo mosaics and blue-and-white Sidi Bou Saïd
- The UNESCO Roman cities of Dougga and Sbeitla
- Holy Kairouan and the colossal El Jem amphitheatre
- Private camel trek into the Sahara and a night in a luxe desert camp
- A night in a restored troglodyte house at Matmata/Tamazret
- Guellala pottery, Djerba weavers and a Berber family home visit

### Day-by-Day Itinerary

**Day 1: Arrival in Tunis & Sidi Bou Saïd**
Private transfer on arrival and a gentle introduction to the capital, ending among the cobbled, bougainvillea-draped lanes of clifftop Sidi Bou Saïd for sunset over the Gulf of Tunis. Settle into a restored boutique *dar*.
*Accommodation:* Dar El Jeld (boutique dar, Tunis) · *Meals:* Dinner

![Boutique dar in Tunis](images/Dareljeld.jpg)

**Day 2: Carthage & the Bardo Museum**
A private day among the Punic and Roman ruins of ancient Carthage, then the Bardo Museum to walk through one of the world's greatest collections of Roman mosaics, with your guide drawing out the stories behind them.
*Accommodation:* Dar El Jeld (boutique dar, Tunis) · *Meals:* Breakfast

![Bardo Museum mosaics](images/bardomuseum.webp)

**Day 3: Dougga & on to Kairouan**
Drive inland to UNESCO-listed Dougga, the best-preserved Roman town in North Africa, set above golden plains. Continue to holy Kairouan, fourth city of Islam.
*Accommodation:* Boutique riad-style stay, Kairouan · *Meals:* Breakfast, Dinner

![Roman ruins of Dougga](images/dougga.webp)

**Day 4: Kairouan & Sbeitla**
Explore Kairouan's Great Mosque, medina and the Aghlabid basins, then the serene Roman temples of Sbeitla rising from the steppe.
*Accommodation:* Boutique stay near Sbeitla · *Meals:* Breakfast

![Kairouan kasbah and medina](images/kasbaah-kairouan.jpg)

**Day 5: Roman temples of Sbeitla & the road south**
A quiet morning among the forum and three temples of Sbeitla before crossing to the date-palm oasis town of Tozeur, gateway to the desert.
*Accommodation:* Tamerza Palace (oasis hotel) · *Meals:* Breakfast, Dinner

![Roman temples at Sbeitla](images/sbeitla.jpg)

**Day 6: Mountain oases & Chott el Jerid**
A 4x4 excursion to the dramatic mountain oases of Chebika and Tamerza with their waterfalls and palmeries, then the shimmering salt flats of Chott el Jerid.
*Accommodation:* Tamerza Palace (oasis hotel) · *Meals:* Breakfast, Dinner

![Mountain oasis](images/mountain-oasis.jpg)

**Day 7: Camel trek & desert camp**
A private camel trek into the dunes (always optional, on welfare-aligned terms) to reach an upscale desert camp, with mint tea, music around the fire and a star-filled Saharan sky.
*Accommodation:* Dunes Insolites (upscale desert camp, luxe en-suite tent) · *Meals:* Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner

![Desert camp under the stars](images/desert-camp-setup.jpg)

**Day 8: Sahara morning & troglodyte Matmata**
Sunrise over the dunes, then travel to the lunar landscape of Matmata/Tamazret for a night in a restored troglodyte house, sharing tea with a Berber host family.
*Accommodation:* Restored troglodyte house, Tamazret · *Meals:* Breakfast, Dinner

![Berber troglodyte village](images/berber-village-1.jpg)

**Day 9: Ksar Hadada & Chenini**
Discover the fortified granary of Ksar Hadada and the cliff-clinging Berber village of Chenini, with sweeping views and a sense of the deep south.
*Accommodation:* Boutique guesthouse, southern Tunisia · *Meals:* Breakfast, Dinner

![Ksar Hadada fortified granary](images/ksar-hadada.jpg)

**Day 10: Djerba — pottery, weavers & island life**
Cross to the island of Djerba: Guellala pottery workshops, Djerba weavers, the open-air street-art quarter of Djerbahood and a stay in a characterful island *dar*.
*Accommodation:* Dar Dhiafa (island dar, Djerba) · *Meals:* Breakfast

![Djerba island](images/djerba.jpeg)

**Day 11: El Jem & Sousse**
North via the staggering Roman amphitheatre of El Jem, then the UNESCO medina and ribat of coastal Sousse.
*Accommodation:* Boutique stay, Sousse/Hammamet · *Meals:* Breakfast

![Sousse medina](images/sousse.webp)

**Day 12: Hammamet & departure**
A relaxed final morning in the gardens and medina of Hammamet before your private transfer to the airport.
*Accommodation:* — · *Meals:* Breakfast

![Hammamet coast](images/hammamet.jpg)

# RESPONSIBLE TRAVEL

Responsibility is built into how we operate, not bolted on:

- **Locally owned, locally enjoyed:** ~95% of accommodation and ~90% of meals are locally owned, keeping spend in Tunisian communities.
- **Fair, direct pay** to the artisans, hosts and families your clients meet — Guellala potters, Djerba weavers, Berber host families.
- **Animal welfare:** camel experiences are always optional and run on ABTA-aligned welfare terms.
- **Lower-impact basics:** reusable water provision and a move away from single-use plastics.
- **Carbon transparency:** indicative CO₂e-per-night figures available, and we are eCollective-ready for clients who wish to offset.

The result is a journey your clients can feel good about — luxury that is also genuinely local and low-impact.
