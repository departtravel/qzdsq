Depart Travel Services is a licensed Tunisian Destination Management Company based in Djerba, in the deep south of the country. We design and operate journeys across the whole of Tunisia — from the Roman cities of the north to the dunes of the Grand Erg Oriental and the Berber *ksour* of the Dahar mountains.

We run our own insured vehicle fleet, work with ONTT-certified national tour leaders, and provide 24/7 in-country support. Whether the brief is a private FIT itinerary, a couple's romantic escape, or a fully tailor-made commission, every booking is handled directly by the people on the ground who will make it happen.

We are an experiential, sustainable and offbeat specialist: characterful local stays, genuine community contact, real food, and soft adventure away from the coach circuit.

## Highlights of Tunisia — 8 Days

### Overview

Our signature introduction to Tunisia, built for couples and immersive first-time FITs. In eight days it moves from the capital's Andalusian and Roman heritage to the Sahara's edge, balancing culture, genuine local contact and one unforgettable romantic night under the desert stars — all in characterful, locally-owned stays.

### Key Information

- **Duration:** 8 days / 7 nights
- **Style:** Private, tailor-made; guaranteed from 2 travellers
- **Guiding:** French-speaking ONTT-certified guides; own insured fleet; 24/7 support
- **Start / End:** Tunis / Djerba (open-jaw friendly)

### Highlights

- A romantic night in a Sahara desert camp under the stars
- Blue-and-white Sidi Bou Saïd and the Bardo Museum
- Guellala pottery and Djerba weavers, paid directly
- A stay in a restored troglodyte guesthouse
- Heritage *dar* accommodation throughout

### Day-by-Day Itinerary

**Day 1: Arrival in Tunis & Sidi Bou Saïd**
Meet your French-speaking guide on arrival and transfer to the clifftop village of Sidi Bou Saïd, with its cobalt doors and Mediterranean views — a gentle, romantic first evening with time for mint tea overlooking the bay.
![Sidi Bou Saïd](images/sidi-bou-said.jpg)
*Accommodation:* Boutique heritage *dar* · *Meals:* Dinner

**Day 2: Carthage & the Bardo Museum**
Explore the ruins of ancient Carthage above the sea, then the Bardo Museum's world-class Roman mosaics, before a relaxed afternoon in Tunis's medina with its artisans and cafés.
![Bardo Museum](images/bardomuseum.webp)
*Accommodation:* Boutique heritage *dar* · *Meals:* Breakfast, dinner

**Day 3: Roman Dougga**
A scenic drive into the hills to UNESCO-listed Dougga, the best-preserved Roman town in North Africa, with its capitol, theatre and temples set among olive groves — unhurried and crowd-free.
![Dougga](images/dougga.webp)
*Accommodation:* Boutique heritage *dar* · *Meals:* Breakfast, dinner

**Day 4: Kairouan**
Travel to the holy city of Kairouan to visit the Great Mosque and the kasbah, and meet local carpet weavers in their workshops, with fair payment direct to the artisans.
![Kasbah, Kairouan](images/kasbaah-kairouan.jpg)
*Accommodation:* Locally-owned hotel · *Meals:* Breakfast, dinner

**Day 5: Into the Sahara — Desert Camp**
Cross to the desert's edge and transfer into the dunes for an optional sunset camel trek, then dinner, Berber music and a romantic night under the stars at our locally-run camp.
![Desert camp under the stars](images/desert-camp-setup.jpg)
*Accommodation:* Dunes Insolites desert camp · *Meals:* Breakfast, lunch, dinner

**Day 6: Mountain Oases & Tamerza**
A soft-adventure day among the mountain oases of Tamerza and Midès, with palm-fringed gorges and waterfalls, ending at the characterful Tamerza Palace.
![Mountain oasis](images/mountain-oasis.jpg)
*Accommodation:* Tamerza Palace · *Meals:* Breakfast, dinner

**Day 7: Matmata Troglodyte Country to Djerba**
Visit the underground troglodyte homes of Matmata and share tea with a Berber family, then cross the causeway to the island of Djerba for your final night.
![Troglodyte Berber village](images/berber-village-1.jpg)
*Accommodation:* Dar Dhiafa, Djerba · *Meals:* Breakfast, dinner

**Day 8: Djerba & Departure**
A morning at the Djerbahood street-art quarter and Guellala pottery before your departure transfer from Djerba airport.
![Djerbahood, Djerba](images/djerbahood.jpg)
*Accommodation:* — · *Meals:* Breakfast

## Berber South & Sahara — 8–9 Days

### Overview

Our most offbeat and experiential journey, designed for couples and soft-adventure travellers who want genuine immersion. It threads the Dahar mountain *ksour*, troglodyte villages and palm oases before a night deep in the Grand Erg Oriental — a route built almost entirely on locally-owned stays and direct community contact.

### Key Information

- **Duration:** 8–9 days / 7–8 nights
- **Style:** Private, tailor-made, soft adventure; guaranteed from 2 travellers
- **Guiding:** French-speaking ONTT-certified guides; own insured fleet; 24/7 support
- **Start / End:** Djerba / Djerba

### Highlights

- A wild bivouac and camel trek in the Grand Erg Oriental
- The Berber *ksour* of Ksar Hadada, Chenini and Douiret
- A night in a restored troglodyte guesthouse
- Mountain-oasis walking at Tamerza and Midès
- Berber family home visit and shared meal

### Day-by-Day Itinerary

**Day 1: Arrival in Djerba**
Meet your French-speaking guide and settle into a heritage island *dar*, with a gentle introduction to Djerba's lanes, light and cuisine.
![Djerba](images/djerba.jpeg)
*Accommodation:* Dar Dhiafa, Djerba · *Meals:* Dinner

**Day 2: Djerba Artisans — Guellala & Weavers**
A hands-on day with Djerba's makers: the potters of Guellala and the island's weavers, paid directly, with time at the Djerbahood street-art quarter.
![Djerbahood, Djerba](images/djerbahood.jpg)
*Accommodation:* Dar Dhiafa, Djerba · *Meals:* Breakfast, dinner

**Day 3: Into the Dahar — Ksar Hadada**
Cross to the mainland and climb into the Dahar mountains to the fortified granary *ksar* of Ksar Hadada, an atmospheric introduction to Berber architecture.
![Ksar Hadada](images/ksar-hadada.jpg)
*Accommodation:* Restored troglodyte guesthouse · *Meals:* Breakfast, lunch, dinner

**Day 4: Chenini, Douiret & Berber Family Home**
Walk the cliff-clinging villages of Chenini and Douiret, then share a home-cooked meal with a Berber family — unhurried, immersive and entirely local.
![Berber mountain village](images/berber-village-2.jpg)
*Accommodation:* Restored troglodyte guesthouse · *Meals:* Breakfast, lunch, dinner

**Day 5: Into the Sahara — Camel Trek & Camp**
Transfer to the dunes for an optional camel trek into the Grand Erg Oriental, arriving at camp for sunset, dinner and Berber music — a romantic night under the stars.
![Desert camp music](images/desert-camp-music.jpg)
*Accommodation:* Dunes Insolites desert camp · *Meals:* Breakfast, lunch, dinner

**Day 6: Dunes Morning & Mountain Oases**
Sunrise over the erg, then a soft-adventure transfer to the mountain oases, walking the palm gorges of Tamerza and Midès.
![Gorge and canyon](images/gorge-canyon.jpg)
*Accommodation:* Tamerza Palace · *Meals:* Breakfast, dinner

**Day 7: Oasis & Tamerza Palace**
A relaxed day among the cascades and palms, with time to swim and rest at Tamerza Palace's oasis pool before the return south.
![Oasis pool](images/oasis-pool.jpg)
*Accommodation:* Tamerza Palace · *Meals:* Breakfast, dinner

**Day 8: Return to Djerba & Departure**
Return across the causeway to Djerba for your departure transfer, or extend with an island night to round off the journey.
![Djerba](images/Dareljeld.jpg)
*Accommodation:* — (or extra night, Dar Dhiafa) · *Meals:* Breakfast

# RESPONSIBLE TRAVEL

Sustainability is the spine of every Depart Travel journey, not an add-on:

- **~95% locally-owned accommodation** and **~90% locally-owned meals**, keeping spend in the communities you visit.
- **Fair, direct payment** to artisans, weavers, potters and host families.
- **ABTA-aligned animal welfare** — all camel experiences are optional and operated to welfare standards.
- **Reusable water** provision to cut single-use plastic across the trip.
- **CO₂e-per-night reporting** and **eCollective-ready** carbon data on request, so Dunia Travel can offset and report with confidence.

For your sustainable, experiential proposition, this means a partner whose practice — not just brochure — matches what you sell to your clients.
