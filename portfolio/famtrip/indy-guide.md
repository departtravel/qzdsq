# WHO WE ARE

Depart Travel Services is a licensed Tunisian Destination Management Company based in Djerba, in the deep south of the country. We design and operate journeys across the whole of Tunisia — from the Roman cities of the north to the dunes of the Grand Erg Oriental and the Berber *ksour* of the Dahar mountains.

We run our **own insured vehicle fleet**, work with **ONTT-certified licensed local guides and drivers**, and provide **24/7 in-country support**. Our team works fluently in English and French. Every component we operate is modular: a single day tour, a point-to-point transfer, an overnight in a locally-owned *dar*, or a full multi-day itinerary — booked directly with the people on the ground who deliver it.

We are an experiential, sustainable and offbeat specialist: characterful local stays, genuine community contact, real food, and soft adventure away from the coach circuit — exactly the kind of vetted local supply an independent-travel platform needs.

## Berber South & Sahara — 8–9 Days

### Overview

Our most offbeat journey, built for seasoned independents who want the real south: troglodyte villages, Dahar mountain *ksour*, palm oases and a genuine night in the Sahara. Operated end to end by our licensed local guides and drivers, with locally-owned stays at every stop.

### Key Information

- **Duration:** 8–9 days / 7–8 nights
- **Style:** Private FIT; fully modular; guaranteed from 2 travellers
- **Guiding:** ONTT-certified licensed local guides & drivers; own insured fleet; 24/7 support
- **Start / End:** Djerba or Tunis (flexible routing)

### Highlights

- A night in a Sahara desert camp near Douz
- Matmata and Tamezret troglodyte villages
- Dahar *ksour* and the mountain oases of Tamerza, Chebika and Midès
- Chott el Jerid salt-lake crossing
- Direct local-contact stays and fair, direct payment to hosts

### Day-by-Day Itinerary

**Day 1: Arrival in Djerba**
Meet your licensed local guide and driver on arrival in Djerba. Settle into a traditional island *dar* and explore the lanes of the Djerbahood street-art quarter at your own pace — an easy, atmospheric first evening on the island.
![Djerbahood](images/djerbahood.jpg)
*Accommodation:* Island *dar* · *Meals:* Dinner

**Day 2: Djerba — El Ghriba & Guellala**
A full day on the island: the historic El Ghriba synagogue, the pottery workshops of Guellala where artisans still throw by hand, and time among Djerba's quiet beaches and palm groves before a relaxed evening.
![Djerba](images/djerba.jpeg)
*Accommodation:* Island *dar* · *Meals:* Breakfast, dinner

**Day 3: Mainland & Ksar Hadada**
Cross to the mainland and climb into the Dahar mountains to the fortified Berber *ksour*, with their honeycomb of mud-brick granaries. A genuinely off-the-beaten-path day among landscapes few travellers reach.
![Ksar Hadada](images/ksar-hadada.jpg)
*Accommodation:* Restored *ksar* guesthouse · *Meals:* Breakfast, dinner

**Day 4: Berber Villages of the Dahar**
A slow day exploring the cliff-edge Berber villages of the Dahar, meeting hosts and walking between hamlets where Amazigh traditions are still lived rather than performed.
![Berber village](images/berber-village-1.jpg)
*Accommodation:* Restored *ksar* guesthouse · *Meals:* Breakfast, dinner

**Day 5: Matmata Troglodyte Villages**
Travel to Matmata to discover the famous underground troglodyte homes, then continue to hillside Tamezret. Tonight you sleep in a troglodyte guesthouse — a quiet, cool, characterful local stay.
![Berber village](images/berber-village-2.jpg)
*Accommodation:* Troglodyte guesthouse · *Meals:* Breakfast, dinner

**Day 6: Douz & the Sahara Camp**
Reach Douz, the gateway to the Sahara, for an afternoon camel trek (always optional, on welfare grounds) into the dunes. Spend the night at a desert camp with local musicians around the fire under a vast sky.
![Desert camp music](images/desert-camp-music.jpg)
*Accommodation:* Desert camp · *Meals:* Breakfast, lunch, dinner

**Day 7: Chott el Jerid & Mountain Oases**
Cross the shimmering Chott el Jerid salt lake to the Jebel en route to the mountain oases. Explore the palm-fringed cascades and pools of Tamerza and Chebika by 4x4, ending at an oasis stay.
![Mountain oasis](images/mountain-oasis.jpg)
*Accommodation:* Oasis hotel · *Meals:* Breakfast, dinner

**Day 8: Midès Canyon & Departure**
A final morning at the dramatic Midès canyon on the Algerian frontier before your transfer to Tozeur or Djerba for onward travel — or extend with any of our modular building blocks.
![Gorge canyon](images/gorge-canyon.jpg)
*Accommodation:* — · *Meals:* Breakfast

## Independent Tunisia (FIT) — ~7 Days

### Overview

A flexible, build-your-own route assembled entirely from our platform-ready modules — ideal for the seasoned independent who wants the country's highlights with room to improvise. Every day is a self-contained block; add, drop or reorder to suit the traveller.

### Key Information

- **Duration:** ~7 days / 6 nights
- **Style:** Fully modular FIT; à-la-carte transfers and accommodation; guaranteed from 2 travellers
- **Guiding:** ONTT-certified licensed local guides & drivers; own insured fleet; 24/7 support
- **Start / End:** Tunis (flexible)

### Highlights

- Carthage, Sidi Bou Saïd and the Bardo's Roman mosaics
- Crowd-free Roman Dougga and the holy city of Kairouan
- El Jem amphitheatre and the Sousse medina
- One night in a Sahara camp near Douz
- Built from listable modules — easy to customise per traveller

### Day-by-Day Itinerary

**Day 1: Arrival in Tunis & Sidi Bou Saïd**
Meet your licensed local guide on arrival and transfer to the clifftop village of Sidi Bou Saïd, with its cobalt doors and Mediterranean views — a gentle, atmospheric first evening with time for mint tea overlooking the bay.
![Sidi Bou Saïd](images/sidi-bou-said.jpg)
*Accommodation:* Boutique heritage *dar* · *Meals:* Dinner

**Day 2: Carthage & the Bardo Museum**
Explore the ruins of ancient Carthage above the sea, then the Bardo Museum's world-class Roman mosaics, before a relaxed afternoon in the Tunis medina among artisans and cafés.
![Carthage](images/carthage.jpg)
*Accommodation:* Boutique heritage *dar* · *Meals:* Breakfast

**Day 3: Roman Dougga**
A scenic drive into the hills to UNESCO-listed Dougga, the best-preserved Roman town in North Africa, with its capitol, theatre and temples set among olive groves — unhurried and crowd-free.
![Dougga](images/dougga.webp)
*Accommodation:* Country guesthouse · *Meals:* Breakfast, dinner

**Day 4: Kairouan & Sbeitla**
Visit Kairouan, the holy city, with its Great Mosque, then continue to the Roman forum and temples of Sbeitla — two of the centre's most rewarding and least-crowded stops.
![Kairouan](images/kasbaah-kairouan.jpg)
*Accommodation:* Town hotel · *Meals:* Breakfast

**Day 5: The Sahara at Douz**
Transfer south to Douz, gateway to the Sahara, for an optional camel trek into the dunes and a night in a desert camp with local hosts and music under the stars.
![Desert camp setup](images/desert-camp-setup.jpg)
*Accommodation:* Desert camp · *Meals:* Breakfast, lunch, dinner

**Day 6: Oasis & El Jem**
Begin among the palm oases before heading north to the colossal El Jem amphitheatre, one of the largest in the Roman world, then on toward the coast.
![Oasis pool](images/oasis-pool.jpg)
*Accommodation:* Coastal *dar* · *Meals:* Breakfast

**Day 7: Sousse & Departure**
A final morning in the UNESCO medina of Sousse — its ribat and kasbah — before your transfer to the airport, or extend with any further module.
![Sousse](images/sousse.webp)
*Accommodation:* — · *Meals:* Breakfast
