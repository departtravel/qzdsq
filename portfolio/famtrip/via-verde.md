# WHO WE ARE

Depart Travel Services is a licensed Tunisian Destination Management Company (DMC) based on the island of Djerba. We design and operate journeys across the whole of Tunisia — from the Mediterranean coast to the mountain oases of the Jebel and the dunes of the Sahara — with our own insured vehicle fleet and ONTT-certified, English-speaking tour leaders (German-speaking guides available on request).

Our model is deliberately small-scale and people-centred. We work hand in hand with the families, artisans, hosts and small hoteliers who make Tunisia worth travelling, and we back every departure with 24/7 in-country support. Whether your clients travel as a couple (FIT), in a small group, or on a fully tailor-made basis, we deliver precise, dependable itineraries with the warmth of genuine local hospitality.

# WHY TUNISIA FOR YOUR TRAVELLERS

Tunisia packs an extraordinary range into a small, walkable country. In a single trip your clients move from Mediterranean medinas and Roman cities to the Berber south, the troglodyte villages of Matmata, the mountain oases of the Jebel, and the silence of the Sahara. Distances are short, the season is long, and direct flights from Germany are frequent and affordable.

For travellers who already love Jordan, Morocco and Oman, Tunisia offers the same draw — desert, hospitality, living craft traditions and layered history — with fewer crowds and an intimacy that suits FIT and small groups. It is, in short, a destination where sustainable, slow, people-first travel comes naturally.

## Walking Tunisia — Coast, Oases & Berber Trails

**Overview.** An eight-day soft-adventure walking journey linking the Mediterranean coast to the canyons and mountain oases of the Tunisian south. Days are paced for comfortable hiking — palm-shaded gorges, escarpment trails and Berber paths — with nights in characterful, locally-owned stays. Ideal for FIT and small groups who want to be active without losing the cultural thread.

**Key Information.**
- Duration: 8 days / 7 nights
- Style: Soft-adventure walking; moderate, 3–5 hours per walking day
- Group size: Guaranteed from 2 passengers; small groups and tailor-made
- Guiding: ONTT-certified English-speaking leader (German-speaking on request)
- Best season: October–April

**Highlights.**
- Hiking the mountain oases and canyons of Chebika, Tamerza and Midès
- Berber trails and palm-grove walks
- A restored troglodyte guesthouse stay
- A night at a Saharan-edge desert camp with Berber music
- Locally-owned *dars* and oasis hotels throughout

**Day-by-Day Itinerary.**

**Day 1: Arrival in Djerba**
Arrive on Djerba island, where our team welcomes you and transfers you to a locally-owned island *dar*. A gentle orientation walk through the village and a welcome dinner set the tone for the week ahead.
![Djerba island arrival](images/djerba.jpeg)
*Accommodation:* Island *dar* (locally owned) · *Meals:* Dinner

**Day 2: Djerba to Matmata — Berber Trails**
Cross to the mainland and into the Berber highlands of Matmata. After lunch with a local family, we walk the ridge trails between troglodyte homesteads, learning how these underground dwellings keep cool in the heat.
![Berber troglodyte village](images/berber-village-2.jpg)
*Accommodation:* Troglodyte guesthouse (homestay-style) · *Meals:* Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner

**Day 3: Matmata to the Mountain Oases**
A scenic transfer brings us to the foot of the Jebel. We hike into our first mountain oasis, walking among date palms and irrigation channels to a viewpoint over the plain below.
![Mountain oasis hiking](images/mountain-oasis.jpg)
*Accommodation:* Oasis hotel (locally owned) · *Meals:* Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner

**Day 4: Chebika & Tamerza Canyons**
The signature walking day: we trek the canyon of Chebika to its waterfall, then continue along the gorge trails toward Tamerza, with a refreshing pause at a natural pool.
![Gorge and canyon trail](images/gorge-canyon.jpg)
*Accommodation:* Tamerza Palace (locally owned) · *Meals:* Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner

**Day 5: Midès Gorge to the Desert Edge**
A morning walk through the dramatic Midès gorge, then a transfer south to the edge of the Sahara. As the sun drops, we settle into a desert camp for an evening of Berber music around the fire.
![Desert camp with Berber music](images/desert-camp-music.jpg)
*Accommodation:* Saharan desert camp · *Meals:* Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner

**Day 6: Desert Morning & Oasis Pools**
An optional sunrise camel walk (entirely your choice), then back to greener country to relax beside spring-fed oasis pools and walk the surrounding palm groves at an easy pace.
![Oasis pool](images/oasis-pool.jpg)
*Accommodation:* Oasis hotel (locally owned) · *Meals:* Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner

**Day 7: Return to the Coast via Tamerza**
A final mountain walk near Tamerza before we make our way back toward the coast, with time to reflect on the week's trails and the communities who shared them.
![Tamerza mountain oasis](images/tamerzapalace.jpg)
*Accommodation:* Heritage *dar* (locally owned) · *Meals:* Breakfast, Dinner

**Day 8: Departure**
A relaxed morning and a last stroll before your transfer to the airport, ending where we began on Djerba.
![Djerba departure](images/Dareljeld.jpg)
*Accommodation:* — · *Meals:* Breakfast

## Berber South & Sahara — Beyond the Map

**Overview.** An eight-to-nine-day immersion in the Berber south: the fortified granaries (ksour) of Ksar Hadada, the cliff villages of Chenini and Douiret, the troglodyte world of Matmata, and a night under the stars in the Sahara. This is deep, people-first travel — homestay-style and troglodyte stays, family meals, and music at the camp — and a perfect companion piece to your Jordan and Oman programmes.

**Key Information.**
- Duration: 8–9 days / 7–8 nights
- Style: Culture, Berber heritage and desert; light walking
- Group size: Guaranteed from 2 passengers; small groups and tailor-made
- Guiding: ONTT-certified English-speaking leader (German-speaking on request)
- Best season: October–April

**Highlights.**
- Ksar Hadada and the ksour of the south
- The cliff-side Berber villages of Chenini and Douiret
- A Berber family home and meal in Matmata
- A restored troglodyte guesthouse stay
- Camel trek and a Saharan desert camp with Berber music

**Day-by-Day Itinerary.**

**Day 1: Arrival in Djerba**
Our team meets you on Djerba and transfers you to a locally-owned island *dar*. Settle in with a welcome dinner and a short walk through the old quarter.
![Djerba arrival](images/djerbahood.jpg)
*Accommodation:* Island *dar* (locally owned) · *Meals:* Dinner

**Day 2: Djerba to Ksar Hadada**
We cross to the mainland and head into ksour country, exploring the vaulted granary cells of Ksar Hadada and learning how these structures protected the harvests — and identities — of southern Berber communities.
![Ksar Hadada granary](images/ksar-hadada.jpg)
*Accommodation:* Heritage *dar* (locally owned) · *Meals:* Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner

**Day 3: Chenini & Douiret Cliff Villages**
A day among the spectacular hilltop villages of Chenini and Douiret, walking the old lanes, visiting a hillside mosque, and meeting residents who still farm the terraced slopes.
![Berber cliff village](images/berber-village-1.jpg)
*Accommodation:* Heritage *dar* (locally owned) · *Meals:* Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner

**Day 4: Into the Sahara — Camel Trek & Camp**
We journey to the dunes for an optional camel trek (always your choice) into the desert, arriving at our camp for tea, sunset over the sand, and a night under a vast sky.
![Sahara dunes](images/dunesinsolites.webp)
*Accommodation:* Saharan desert camp · *Meals:* Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner

**Day 5: Desert Camp & Berber Music**
A morning to absorb the desert's quiet, then an evening of shared food and Berber music around the fire — one of the trip's most memorable nights.
![Desert camp and music](images/desert-camp-setup.jpg)
*Accommodation:* Saharan desert camp · *Meals:* Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner

**Day 6: Sahara to Matmata**
We travel to Matmata, the famous troglodyte landscape, where we are welcomed into a Berber family home for tea and a home-cooked meal, and learn the rhythms of underground living.
![Matmata troglodyte village](images/berber-village-2.jpg)
*Accommodation:* Troglodyte guesthouse (homestay-style) · *Meals:* Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner

**Day 7: Matmata & the Oasis Country**
A morning walk among the troglodyte dwellings, then on to greener oasis country to walk the palm groves and rest beside spring-fed pools.
![Mountain oasis](images/mountain-oasis.jpg)
*Accommodation:* Oasis hotel (locally owned) · *Meals:* Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner

**Day 8: Return to Djerba**
We make our way back toward the coast and Djerba, with a farewell dinner in a locally-owned *dar* to close the journey.
![Djerba return](images/Dareljeld.jpg)
*Accommodation:* Island *dar* (locally owned) · *Meals:* Breakfast, Dinner

**Day 9: Departure**
A final morning at leisure before your transfer to Djerba airport. (Programme can be run as 8 days by combining Days 8–9.)

# RESPONSIBLE TRAVEL

Responsible travel is not an add-on for us — it is how the trips are built. Around 95% of the accommodation we use is locally owned, and roughly 90% of meals are taken in locally-owned establishments, so spending stays in the communities your travellers visit. We pay artisans and hosts directly and fairly: the Guellala potters, the Djerba weavers, the harissa and olive-oil producers, and the Matmata families who open their homes all receive a fair, direct share.

We follow ABTA-aligned welfare standards — every camel ride is optional and never a condition of the experience — and we reduce waste with reusable water solutions in the field. For your reporting, we provide CO2e-per-night figures and are eCollective-ready, so Via Verde's sustainability commitments carry through to the ground in Tunisia.
