# WHO WE ARE

Depart Travel Services is a licensed Tunisian Destination Management Company (DMC) based on the island of Djerba. We design and operate journeys across the whole of Tunisia — from the Mediterranean coast and the Andalusian craft towns to the mountain oases of the Jebel and the dunes of the Sahara — with our own insured vehicle fleet and ONTT-certified tour leaders. **Spanish-speaking guides are available on request**, and every departure is backed by 24/7 in-country support.

Our model is deliberately small-scale and people-centred. We work hand in hand with the families, farmers, artisans, hosts and small hoteliers who make Tunisia worth travelling — paying them directly and fairly. Whether your clients travel as a family, a small private group, or on a fully tailor-made basis, we deliver precise, dependable itineraries with the warmth of genuine local hospitality and gentle, family-friendly pacing.

Tunisia packs an extraordinary range into a small, walkable country. ## Flavours of Tunisia — Culinary & Cultural

### Overview
A 7–8 day journey built around the table. Your families cook with local hosts, meet harissa and olive-oil producers, explore the Andalusian craft town of Testour and the fusion kitchens of Djerba, and sleep in boutique and heritage stays throughout. Farm-to-table, hands-on, and deeply rooted in the rural communities that grow and make the food.

### Key Information
- **Duration:** 7–8 days / 6–7 nights
- **Pacing:** Gentle, family-friendly; interactive workshops daily
- **Guaranteed from:** 2 passengers · private & tailor-made available
- **Guiding:** ONTT-certified; Spanish-speaking on request
- **Style:** Boutique & heritage accommodation, family kitchens, regenerative

### Highlights
- Hands-on cooking workshops in family kitchens
- Harissa producers and traditional olive-oil mills
- Andalusian-Tunisian Testour and Djerban fusion cuisine
- Guellala pottery and Djerba weavers
- Oasis palm-grove visit and farm-to-table meals

### Day-by-Day Itinerary

**Day 1: Arrival in Djerba**
Arrive on the island of Djerba and transfer to a locally-owned heritage *dar*. After settling in, a gentle orientation walk introduces the island's whitewashed lanes before a welcome dinner of Djerban specialities — the first taste of Tunisia's layered culinary heritage.
![Djerba island](images/djerba.jpeg)
*Accommodation:* Dar Dhiafa (boutique heritage *dar*) · *Meals:* Dinner

**Day 2: Djerba — Pottery, Weaving & Fusion Kitchens**
A full day exploring Djerba's craft and culinary traditions: the pottery workshops of Guellala and the island's weavers, with fair, direct payment to the makers. In the afternoon, a hands-on cooking session reveals the island's Jewish, Berber and Arab fusion flavours, followed by a shared family meal.
![Djerba craft quarter](images/djerbahood.jpg)
*Accommodation:* Dar Dhiafa (boutique heritage *dar*) · *Meals:* Breakfast, Lunch

**Day 3: Mainland Oases & Olive Mills**
Cross to the mainland through groves of ancient olive trees, stopping at a traditional family olive-oil mill to see the pressing and taste the new oil. Continue to an oasis where a palm-grove walk explains the three-tier oasis agriculture that has fed these communities for centuries.
![Mountain oasis](images/mountain-oasis.jpg)
*Accommodation:* Oasis boutique hotel · *Meals:* Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner

**Day 4: Harissa & Farm-to-Table**
Morning with a harissa producer — sun-dried chillies, garlic and spice pounded the traditional way — before a farm-to-table lunch prepared with the day's harvest. The afternoon is spent at leisure or with an optional bread-baking session in an oasis kitchen.
![Oasis pool](images/oasis-pool.jpg)
*Accommodation:* Oasis boutique hotel · *Meals:* Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner

**Day 5: Kairouan — Spice, Sweets & the Holy City**
Travel to Kairouan, exploring its kasbah and medina, the makroudh sweet-makers and the spice markets. A guided market visit gathers ingredients for an afternoon cooking workshop, ending in a shared meal with the host family.
![Kasbah of Kairouan](images/kasbaah-kairouan.jpg)
*Accommodation:* Heritage *dar* · *Meals:* Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner

**Day 6: Testour — Andalusian Tunisia**
A day in Testour, founded by Andalusian Muslims expelled from Spain — a heritage your Spanish travellers will recognise. Explore the distinctive architecture and Moorish kitchen traditions, with a cooking session that blends Andalusian and Tunisian techniques.
![Sidi Bou Said](images/sidi-bou-said.jpg)
*Accommodation:* Boutique *dar* · *Meals:* Breakfast, Lunch

**Day 7: Tunis & Departure**
A final morning in the medina of Tunis — its food stalls, spice souks and the Bardo Museum's mosaics depicting Roman feasts — before transferring for your onward flight. Optional extension to the desert glamping camp available.
![Bardo Museum mosaics](images/bardomuseum.webp)
*Accommodation:* — · *Meals:* Breakfast

## Highlights of Tunisia — with Desert Glamping & Berber Heritage

### Overview
An 8-day loop that pairs Tunisia's cultural highlights with the experiences this buyer most wants: desert glamping under the stars, a restored troglodyte stay, Berber family encounters and farm-to-table meals. Boutique accommodation throughout, gentle family pacing, and a strong regenerative footprint.

### Key Information
- **Duration:** 8 days / 7 nights
- **Pacing:** Gentle, family-friendly; one overnight in en-suite desert tents
- **Guaranteed from:** 2 passengers · private & tailor-made available
- **Guiding:** ONTT-certified; Spanish-speaking on request
- **Style:** Boutique & heritage stays, desert glamping, Berber heritage, regenerative

### Highlights
- En-suite desert glamping at Dunes Insolites with camel trek
- Restored troglodyte house and Berber family home in Matmata
- Mountain oases, gorges and the ksour of the south
- Carthage, Sidi Bou Said and the Bardo mosaics
- Olive-oil and harissa tastings; farm-to-table dinners

### Day-by-Day Itinerary

**Day 1: Arrival in Tunis & Sidi Bou Said**
Arrive in Tunis and transfer to the blue-and-white cliff village of Sidi Bou Said for your first evening. A relaxed welcome dinner overlooking the Gulf of Tunis sets a gentle tone for the days ahead.
![Sidi Bou Said](images/sidi-bou-said.jpg)
*Accommodation:* Boutique *dar*, Sidi Bou Said · *Meals:* Dinner

**Day 2: Carthage & the Bardo**
A morning among the ruins of ancient Carthage, then the Bardo Museum's world-class Roman mosaics — many depicting food, farming and feasting, a vivid link to Tunisia's culinary heritage. Afternoon at leisure for families.
![Carthage ruins](images/carthage.jpg)
*Accommodation:* Boutique *dar*, Sidi Bou Said · *Meals:* Breakfast

**Day 3: Coast to Kairouan**
Travel south via the medina of Sousse to the holy city of Kairouan, with its kasbah, Great Mosque and sweet-makers. A market visit and short cooking demonstration introduce the spices of the interior.
![Sousse medina](images/sousse.webp)
*Accommodation:* Heritage *dar*, Kairouan · *Meals:* Breakfast, Dinner

**Day 4: Matmata — Berber & Troglodyte Heritage**
Into the Berber south to Matmata, where families live in underground troglodyte houses carved into the hillsides. Share tea and a home-cooked meal with a Berber family, then overnight in a restored troglodyte guesthouse — an unforgettable, authentic stay.
![Berber village](images/berber-village-1.jpg)
*Accommodation:* Restored troglodyte guesthouse · *Meals:* Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner

**Day 5: Ksour & the Edge of the Sahara — Desert Glamping**
Visit the fortified granaries (ksour) of Ksar Hadada and the Berber village of Tamazret before reaching the Dunes Insolites desert camp. An optional sunset camel trek, dinner under the stars and Berber music around the fire — glamping in en-suite tents on the edge of the Sahara.
![Glamping under the stars](images/desert-camp-setup.jpg)
*Accommodation:* Dunes Insolites desert camp (en-suite tents) · *Meals:* Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner

**Day 6: Mountain Oases & Gorges**
Leave the dunes for the dramatic mountain oases of the Jebel — palm-shaded gorges, waterfalls and canyon walks. A farm-to-table lunch in an oasis kitchen showcases ingredients grown in the palm groves.
![Gorge and canyon](images/gorge-canyon.jpg)
*Accommodation:* Tamerza Palace (oasis hotel) · *Meals:* Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner

**Day 7: Oasis to Djerba**
A relaxed morning at the oasis before crossing to the island of Djerba. En route, stop at an olive-oil mill and a harissa producer for tastings, with fair direct payment to producers. Evening at leisure in a boutique island *dar*.
![Tamerza Palace oasis hotel](images/tamerzapalace.jpg)
*Accommodation:* Dar Dhiafa (boutique heritage *dar*) · *Meals:* Breakfast, Lunch

**Day 8: Djerba & Departure**
A final morning visiting Guellala's potters and Djerba's weavers — buying direct from the makers — before transferring to the airport for your onward flight.
![Djerba island](images/djerba.jpeg)
*Accommodation:* — · *Meals:* Breakfast

# REGENERATIVE TRAVEL

For Depart Travel Services, sustainability is not a footnote — it is how the trips are built, and it is designed to leave rural Tunisian communities measurably better off.

- **Local ownership.** Around 95% of the accommodation we use is locally owned, and roughly 90% of meals are taken in family kitchens and locally-owned establishments — so your clients' spending stays in the villages, oases and islands that host them.
- **Fair, direct pay.** We pay artisans, farmers, cooks and hosts directly and fairly — the harissa producer, the olive miller, the Berber family in Matmata, the Guellala potter. Tourism becomes a reliable income for rural households.
- **Rural-community support.** Itineraries are deliberately routed through small inland and southern communities that see fewer visitors, spreading benefit beyond the coastal resorts.
- **Animal welfare.** ABTA-aligned practice; every camel ride is optional and never a condition of the experience.
- **Lower footprint.** Reusable water solutions, CO2e-per-night figures, and eCollective-ready reporting so Journey Travel can account for the carbon of every trip with confidence.

This is regenerative tourism your families can feel good about — and a credible sustainability story for a new operator to build a brand around.
