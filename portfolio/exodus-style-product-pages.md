## Overview
**Roman ruins without the crowds, the Berber south and a night in the Sahara — the complete Tunisia in one compact loop.**

Over twelve immersive days we trace every layer of Tunisia's story: the mosaics of the Bardo and the ruins of Carthage, the crowd-free Roman cities of Dougga and Sbeitla, the holy city of Kairouan, and the colossal amphitheatre of El Jem. We cross the Chott el Jerid salt lake to ride a camel into the dunes and sleep under the stars, meet Berber hosts in the troglodyte villages of the south, and discover the living Jewish-Berber heritage of Djerba — before finishing on the Mediterranean coast at Sousse and Hammamet. Short transfers and uncrowded sites make this one of the most rewarding and relaxed cultural journeys in North Africa.

**One of our most distinctive trips for 2026 — here's why.** While everyone else queues in Morocco and Jordan, Tunisia delivers the same desert romance and even richer Roman heritage with a fraction of the crowds, on a small, walkable footprint. — *Depart Travel Services*

## Itinerary

**Day 1: Adventure starts in Tunis**
Arrival transfer and evening welcome dinner in a restored medina townhouse.
*Accommodation:* Dar El Jeld / Villa Bleue (or similar) · *Meals:* Dinner

**Day 2: Tunis Medina, Bardo Museum, Carthage & Sidi Bou Saïd**
Guided medina walk, the world-class Bardo mosaics, the ruins of Carthage and sunset in blue-and-white Sidi Bou Saïd.
*Accommodation:* Dar El Jeld / Villa Bleue (or similar) · *Meals:* Breakfast

**Day 3: Testour & Dougga • To Kairouan**
The best-preserved Roman town in North Africa, then the holy city of Kairouan — old medina and Makroudh pastry.
*Accommodation:* Kasbah Kairouan 5★ (or similar) · *Meals:* Breakfast, Lunch

**Day 4: Kairouan & Sbeitla • To Tozeur**
Great Mosque of Kairouan, the Roman forum of Sbeitla, then on to the oasis town of Tozeur.
*Accommodation:* The Mora / Dar Tozeur (or similar) · *Meals:* Breakfast, Lunch

**Day 5: Mountain oases & canyons**
4x4 to Chebika, Tamerza and Midès; optional Mos Espa film set; Tozeur old town.
*Accommodation:* The Mora / Dar Tozeur (or similar) · *Meals:* Breakfast

**Day 6: Chott el Jerid • Camp in the Sahara**
Cross the salt lake to Douz and the Bedouin museum, then camel ride into the dunes and a night under the stars.
*Accommodation:* Sabria / Dunes Insolites desert camp (or similar) · *Meals:* Breakfast, Dinner

**Day 7: Matmata, Tataouine & Chenini**
Tamazret Berber museum, troglodyte homes, the ksour and the hilltop village of Chenini.
*Accommodation:* Ksar Hadada (or similar) · *Meals:* Breakfast

**Day 8: Djerba island (cultural)**
The El Ghriba synagogue, Houmt Souk, Guellala pottery and the Djerbahood street-art quarter.
*Accommodation:* Dar Dhiafa (or similar) · *Meals:* Breakfast

**Day 9: Free day on Djerba**
A free day — optional private boat trip or a full cultural day around the island.
*Accommodation:* Dar Dhiafa (or similar) · *Meals:* Breakfast

**Day 10: El Jem & Sousse**
The colossal Roman amphitheatre of El Jem, then the UNESCO medina and Ribat of Sousse.
*Accommodation:* Dar Badiaa (or similar) · *Meals:* Breakfast, Lunch

**Day 11: Hammamet**
The Kasbah and Andalusian quarter; afternoon at leisure, optional hammam or vineyard wine tasting; farewell dinner.
*Accommodation:* Villa Zembra (or similar) · *Meals:* Breakfast, Dinner

**Day 12: Adventure ends in Tunis**
Departure transfer to Enfidha–Hammamet or Tunis–Carthage airport.
*Meals:* Breakfast
